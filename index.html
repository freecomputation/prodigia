PRODIGIA

A register of anomalous sightings, kept from public reports.

The unit of this register is the event, not the post. Reports of one event from several sources are filed under a single entry, so an entry shows how many independent reports the event has gathered. Each entry carries a standing, which is a statement about the record and never about the phenomenon: UNRESOLVED means the filed reports hold no established ordinary explanation, RESOLVED - ORDINARY means an ordinary cause is established in the record and is named, and INSUFFICIENT RECORD means the filed reports lack the time, place or detail a check requires.
